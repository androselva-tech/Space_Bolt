BASA

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026 · App: BASA (com.androselva1988.spacebolt)

The short version

  • BASA has no accounts, no sign-in, and no servers of ours that collect your information.
  • Your progress (coins, stars, upgrades, answers) stays on your device.
  • The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay. The ad companies it works with may collect device and advertising information, as described below.
  • We ask for your birth year once, only to decide which kind of ads to show. We do not keep the year, only whether you are 13 or under.
  • Players 13 or under get ads set up for children: no personalised ads.

1. Who we are

BASA (“the game”, “we”, “us”) is a science learning game published by Bolt Studio. You build a rocket by answering science questions and fly it to the Moon and back. You can reach us at androselva@gmail.com.

2. Information the game keeps on your device

To let you carry on where you stopped, the game saves this on your phone or tablet:

  • your coins, stars, stages finished and hangar upgrades;
  • your rocket’s name and colours (the name is whatever you type; please don’t use your real name);
  • which questions you answered right or wrong, and your accuracy in each subject, so the game can adjust the questions and bring back ones you missed;
  • whether you are 13 or under (see “Age check” below).

This information is not sent to us. It stays on your device until you reset your progress in the game (Profile, then Reset progress) or uninstall the game. Resetting progress keeps your age answer; the Profile screen has a Change age button.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store personal information about you. The game has no account, sign-in, chat, or social features, and we do not run any analytics or tracking of our own. Google Play may give us anonymous crash and performance statistics for the game through the Play Console.

4. Age check

When you first open the game it asks which year you were born. We use your answer only to decide how ads are set up (see below). The game keeps one thing: whether you are 13 or under. It does not keep your birth year.

5. Advertising and our ad partner

BASA is free and shows ads. Full-screen ads can appear between stages, and you can choose to watch a video ad to get a free hint. The ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, a service that connects the game to advertising companies (“ad partners”).

To show and measure ads, LevelPlay and its ad partners may collect information from your device. This happens inside their software, not through us. It can include:

InformationWhy
Advertising ID (a resettable identifier from Google Play services)To choose and measure ads, and to limit how often one ad is shown
IP address and an approximate location worked out from itTo show ads suited to your country, and to prevent fraud
Device and app details (model, operating system, language, app version)To show ads that work on your device and fix problems
Ad activity (ads loaded, shown, closed or tapped)To count ads, pay for them, and measure how well they work

Players 13 or under. For anyone who says they are 13 or under, the game tells LevelPlay the player is a child, switches off personalised advertising, opts the device out of device identifiers, and marks the data as not to be sold. These players see ads that are not based on their interests.

Everyone else. Ads may be personalised using the advertising ID and the information above, according to the choices you have made in your device and where the law allows it.

The information collected by LevelPlay and its ad partners is handled under their own privacy policies, for example Unity’s Privacy Policy. We recommend reading them.

6. Controlling ads on your device

  • Android: open Settings, then Google (or Privacy), then Ads. There you can delete your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • You can also use your device settings to stop apps connecting to the internet. The game still works without it, but ads will not show.

7. Children’s privacy

BASA is a learning game that children and adults can both enjoy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the game does not ask for names, emails, photos or contact details. Players 13 or under see ads set up for children as described above. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, please contact us at androselva@gmail.com.

8. Permissions the game uses

  • Internet access: needed to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by ad partners for ads, as described above. It is switched off for players 13 or under.

The game does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location.

9. Sharing your information

We do not sell or share personal information that we hold, because we do not hold any. As explained in section 5, the ad partners reached through LevelPlay may collect information directly from your device.

10. Where information is processed

LevelPlay and its ad partners may process information in countries other than your own. They are responsible for protecting it under their own policies.

11. Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete everything the game stores on your device by resetting your progress or uninstalling the game. For information held by the ad partners, use the controls in section 6, or follow the links in their privacy policies. Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or California), you may have extra rights. To ask about them, contact us at androselva@gmail.com and we will help where we can.

12. Security

Your game progress stays on your device and is protected by your device’s own security. Ads are loaded over secure connections where the ad partners support them.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add accounts or change how ads work. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will update the game’s store listing or tell you in the game.
